Quick start

Type chord symbols in any key (D-7 G7 | Cmaj7 | Bmin7b5 E7 | Amin7) or roman numerals (ii-7 V7 | Imaj7). Bars split with |, % repeats a bar. Slash chords work: Cmaj7/G, D-7/G; with numerals, V7/ii means V7 of ii. Bare numerals: lowercase is min7, uppercase is 7 (I and IV are maj7). Plain 7 chords follow the Dominants setting.
